## PRIMEUNITS PLATFORM VISION

PrimeUnits is a Philippine marketplace and business platform focused on:

-   Cars
-   Motorcycles
-   Commercial Vehicles
-   Agricultural Equipment
-   Heavy Equipment
-   Electric Vehicles
-   Boats
-   Industrial Equipment
-   Generators
-   Mobility Services

PrimeUnits is NOT a real estate platform.

PrimeUnits is a marketplace and lead-generation ecosystem for vehicle
owners, dealers, equipment suppliers, rental operators, financing
providers, and related service businesses.
